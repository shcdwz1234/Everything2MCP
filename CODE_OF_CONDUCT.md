# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Everything2MCP a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, trolling, or personal attacks
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainer (**shui**). The maintainer will review and respond as appropriate, including temporary or permanent bans from project spaces.
